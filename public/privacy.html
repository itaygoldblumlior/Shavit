Your clothes. Your device.

Shavit privacy notice · 7 October 2026

Shavit is a personal wardrobe tool by Itay. It has no signup, advertising, or analytics SDKs.

What stays on your device

Your clothing photos, clothing names and colours, saved outfits, music links and mood tags stay in local storage. The website uses your browser’s storage. The mobile app uses private device files and preferences. We do not upload your wardrobe or photos to a Shavit server.

Photos and camera

You choose which photo to add, or take a clothing photo in the mobile app. Background removal and cutout editing run on your device. Camera and photo access are used only when you choose those actions. You can change permissions in your device settings.

Internet connections

The website hosting provider processes normal connection information, such as your IP address, to deliver the site. Automatic background removal downloads model and runtime files from IMG.LY; that provider receives normal connection information, but not your clothing photos. Saved music links open another service when you tap them, and that service has its own privacy policy. Music moods are tags you choose, not audio analysis.

Backups and support

Exporting a backup creates a file containing your clothes, saved fits and music links. You decide where to save or share it. Anyone who receives it can read its contents. Device or browser backups may also retain local data according to your operating system and settings. Android app automatic backup is disabled.

If you email support, we receive the message and any attachments you choose to send. Do not include private information you do not want to share.

Deleting data

Remove clothing, saved outfits and music from their lists. Clearing website storage or uninstalling the mobile app removes its local data; backups you made are separate and must be deleted where you saved them. Export a backup before switching browsers or moving to the mobile app.
