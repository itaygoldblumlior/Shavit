Your day. Your device.

Updated 15 November 2026

Shavit is a personal planner and workout journal. No account, advertising or analytics SDKs.

Stored on this device

Schedules, tasks, workout plans, sets, weights, reps and completed sessions are saved in browser IndexedDB or private native app preferences. They are not uploaded to Shavit servers. The website and installed app have separate data.

Reminders

The installed app can request notification permission to schedule a daily 9 AM notification locally. No remote push service is used. Turn it off in More or device settings.

Your browser

Use the same browser on the same device to return to your records. Private browsing, clearing site data or switching devices can make your records unavailable. Export a backup in More regularly. Browser records do not automatically sync with the iPhone app. Calendar connection and background reminders require the iPhone app.

Imports and backups

Text notes and JSON files are processed locally. Exported backup files contain your personal records; keep them somewhere private. They are shared only through actions you choose.

Internet and deletion

Hosting receives ordinary connection information to serve the website. The installed app works without a connection. Clearing site data or uninstalling the app removes local records; separately saved backups remain. The previous clothing interface is no longer part of this version.
