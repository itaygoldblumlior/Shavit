A little help from your friend.

Updated 27 October 2026

Your first day

Build your week with any split you like: Upper/Lower, full body, or custom names. Use Add activity to schedule both a Lift and a Run on one day. Empty days are rest days. Then open Workouts to create a lifting plan. Plan names must match your weekly schedule names. In Today, open the workout, record kg and reps, and check each completed set. Finish saves it to Progress. Previous results appear alongside the next session of each exercise.

Import your notes

Workouts → Import a text note accepts a .txt file. You can also paste a note into Create workout plan. Write one exercise per line, followed by weight × reps, such as Bench press 40x8 60x6 60x6. First sets are warm-ups by default; edit set types in the workout.

Your private workout file

More → Import backup / workout file accepts a Shavit helper JSON file. Review its summary, then merge plans and history. Matching existing dates are kept.

Streak and reminders

Rest days protect your streak. All scheduled activities must be completed to count a training day. A missed lift or run breaks the streak after that day ends. Streaks begin when you set up your schedule. Daily 9 AM background notifications require the installed iPhone app and notification permission.

Build a plan without a note

Open Workouts → Create plan and give it a name. Save it, then add exercises individually with their set counts. Search plans by name or exercise. New workout sessions use your last completed weights and reps, with all completion checks reset.

Getting around

Use Calendar on Today to jump to any date. In Progress, filter All, Lifting or Runs. Task and exercise removals offer an Undo button. The save message confirms changes are stored on this device.

Track your runs

Open a Run activity and enter distance in km plus separate minutes and seconds, such as 14 minutes and 30 seconds. Shavit saves your exact time and pace per km. The next run at the same distance shows your earlier time and how much faster or slower it was. Completed runs also appear in Progress.

Backups

Export regularly in More. Website and native app data do not sync. Keep your backup when moving phones or reinstalling.
