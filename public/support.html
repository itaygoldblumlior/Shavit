Bring your wardrobe into the app

On the website, open Wardrobe → Help & backups → Export wardrobe. Save the JSON backup file. In the app, open the same menu and choose Import wardrobe, then select your file. Import adds items to your wardrobe; it does not clear existing items.

Background removal

The first use needs an internet connection to download the background remover. If automatic removal cannot finish, tap Fix cutout to erase or restore parts with your finger. You can also keep the original photo.

Keep a backup

Your wardrobe is stored locally. It is not automatically synced between the app and website, or between devices. Export before uninstalling the app or clearing browser storage.

Music and colours

Tap Colours to match a whole outfit. Tap the music note below Save fit to save a song or playlist link with its mood. “Dress for this song” chooses clothes using that tag.
